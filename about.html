About YAMER

Overview

YAMER — the Yashoda Academy of Medical Education and Research — is a dedicated institution committed to advancing excellence in medical education, health research, innovation, and scientific publication. It serves as an integrated platform that brings together researchers, clinicians, academicians, students, industry partners, and regulatory bodies to foster multidisciplinary collaboration, promote evidence-based healthcare, and translate scientific discoveries into improved patient and population health.

Mission

Advancing healthcare through research

To promote, facilitate, and strengthen high-quality medical and health research by fostering scientific excellence, ethical research practices, interdisciplinary collaboration, innovation, and capacity building. YAMER is committed to supporting the complete research ecosystem—from concept to translation—through robust governance, advanced infrastructure, education, partnerships, and community engagement, thereby generating evidence that improves clinical practice, healthcare delivery, and population health.

Vision

A leading academic research institution

To lead transformative medical and health research through scientific excellence, innovation, collaboration, and ethical leadership, creating lasting improvements in healthcare and society.
