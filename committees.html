YAMER

Every research activity at YAMER passes through — the Institutional Ethics Committee, it reviews the ethics of individual studies, while the Research Advisory Board sets and reviews overall research strategy and scientific quality.

Review Process

Ethics review workflow

Submitted protocols undergo an administrative completeness check before being scheduled for committee review.

The full committee evaluates scientific merit, risk-benefit balance, and informed consent procedures.

Approved studies are subject to periodic continuing review until study closure.
